LeetCode 1011 (Ship Within D Days)

We are finding the smallest valid capacity.

Example:

capacity = 10 ✗
capacity = 11 ✗
capacity = 12 ✓
capacity = 13 ✓
capacity = 14 ✓

At the end of binary search:

high = 11   (last invalid)
low  = 12   (first valid)

Visualization:

10 ✗   11 ✗   12 ✓   13 ✓   14 ✓
         ↑      ↑
       high    low

Pattern:

F F T T T

Code:

if(isPossible(mid))
    high = mid - 1;   // try smaller capacity
else
    low = mid + 1;    // need larger capacity

After the loop:

high = last invalid
low  = first valid

So we return:

return low;

Pattern:

T T F F F

if(valid(mid))
    low = mid + 1;
else

After the loop:

high = last valid
low  = first invalid

So we return:
